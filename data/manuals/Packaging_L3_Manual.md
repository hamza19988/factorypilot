# Packaging Line L3 — Operator & Maintenance Manual (Rev. 3)

## p.8 — Operating envelope
Line speed 30 m/min. Sealer motor temperature 50–60 °C. Drive current 16–20 A. Vibration below 2.8 mm/s RMS.

## p.19 — Seal defects
Incomplete or wrinkled seals are caused by sealing jaw temperature drift or contaminated jaws. Clean jaws with a brass brush and verify jaw temperature with a contact probe (target 165 °C ±5 °C).

## p.24 — Fasteners and missing components
Missing or loose fasteners on the carton erector frame cause rattling (vibration spikes) and misaligned folds. Check all M8 frame bolts for presence and torque (22 Nm) weekly. A missing bolt must be replaced before the next production run.

## p.30 — Dents and crushed packages
Dents on packages indicate a jammed accumulation table or excessive back-pressure. Reduce accumulation pressure and check photo-eye timing.

## p.36 — Safety
LOTO on isolator Q3. Heated jaws remain hot for 20 minutes after shutdown.
