# CNC Machining Center M4 — Maintenance Guide (Rev. 7)

## p.14 — Operating envelope
Spindle motor temperature 45–60 °C. Spindle current 15–20 A at typical load. Spindle vibration below 2.8 mm/s RMS. Feed rate as programmed (line_speed channel reports table feed in m/min).

## p.27 — Surface finish defects and chatter
Chatter marks (regular wavy pattern) and scratches on machined faces are caused by worn tooling, insufficient workholding rigidity or spindle bearing wear. A rising vibration trend together with degraded surface finish indicates spindle bearing preload loss. Replace the cutting insert first; if marks persist, run the spindle run-out check (p.29).

## p.29 — Spindle run-out check
Mount a test bar and dial indicator. Run-out above 5 µm at 300 mm requires spindle bearing service by a certified technician.

## p.33 — Coolant and corrosion
Coolant concentration below 5 % (refractometer) leads to flash rust on finished parts and on the machine table within hours. Orange-brown surface staining on steel parts indicates corrosion; check coolant concentration and pH (target 8.8–9.4). Replace coolant if bacterial contamination is detected (odour, pH below 8.5).

## p.45 — Safety
Door interlocks must never be bypassed. LOTO on isolator Q4 before tool or spindle work.
