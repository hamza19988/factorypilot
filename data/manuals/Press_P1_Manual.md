# Hydraulic Press P1 — Service Manual (Rev. 2)

## p.9 — Operating envelope
Nominal stroke rate 30 strokes/min. Main motor temperature 50–60 °C. Main pump motor current 16–20 A. Frame vibration below 2.8 mm/s RMS. Hydraulic oil temperature must remain below 60 °C.

## p.22 — Die cracks and part cracking
Hairline cracks on stamped parts that repeat in the same location point to a cracked or chipped die insert. Cracks that vary in position point to material issues (coil hardness, lubrication). A cracked die insert must be removed from service immediately: continued use can cause insert fragmentation. This is a safety-critical condition requiring supervisor sign-off before restart.

## p.23 — Die lubrication
Insufficient die lubrication increases forming force, raises motor current by 10–25 % and causes galling and surface tearing. Check lubricant reservoir level and spray nozzles for clogging every shift.

## p.31 — Hydraulic pump wear
Pump wear shows as rising motor current, rising oil temperature and slower stroke rate. Increased vibration with a whining noise indicates cavitation; check suction strainer and oil level first.

## p.40 — Safety
Press work requires LOTO on isolator Q2 and mechanical die blocks inserted. Light curtain must be tested at the start of every shift.
