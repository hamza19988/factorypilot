# Conveyor C2 — Operation & Maintenance Manual (Rev. 4)

## p.12 — Normal operating envelope
Nominal line speed is 30 m/min (±1 m/min). Drive motor temperature in steady state is 52–58 °C. Motor current at nominal load is 17–19 A. Vibration velocity measured at the drive-end bearing must stay below 2.8 mm/s RMS (ISO 10816-3 zone B boundary). Values between 2.8 and 4.5 mm/s RMS indicate developing faults; above 4.5 mm/s RMS the conveyor must be scheduled for stop within the shift; above 7.1 mm/s RMS stop immediately.

## p.37 — Guide rail pads and part scratching
Polymer guide rail pads (part no. GRP-220) keep parts centered during transfer. When pad wear exceeds 1 mm, the steel rail backing can contact the part and leave longitudinal scratches on the upper surface, typically on the side nearest the worn pad. Inspect pads with a feeler gauge every 250 operating hours. Replace both pads of a pair at the same time. Torque fixing screws to 8 Nm. After replacement, run 20 parts and inspect for surface marks.

## p.38 — Belt tracking and misalignment
A belt running off-center increases lateral load on the guide rails, accelerates pad wear and raises drive current by 10–20 %. Symptoms: rising current_a with stable line speed, intermittent edge scratches, belt edge fraying. Correct tracking with the tail-pulley adjusters in quarter-turn steps while running at low speed.

## p.41 — Drive-end bearing failure
Bearing wear produces a progressive rise in vibration RMS, often accelerating (quadratic trend) over hours, accompanied by a motor temperature rise of 2–4 °C per hour and isolated vibration spikes from rolling-element impacts. Re-grease with 6 g of lithium complex grease (NLGI 2). If vibration remains above 4.5 mm/s RMS after re-greasing, replace bearing 6308-2RS. Lock out / tag out (LOTO) the drive before any bearing work.

## p.44 — Reject rate escalation
A reject rate above 2 % at the vision station is usually caused by surface damage introduced upstream (guide rails, transfer wheels) rather than by the camera. Check guide rail pads (p.37) and belt tracking (p.38) before recalibrating the camera.

## p.52 — Safety
All maintenance requires LOTO on the main isolator Q1. Never reach across a running belt. Two-person rule applies to bearing replacement.
